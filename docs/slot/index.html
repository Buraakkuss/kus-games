Slot

One tap. Turn the shape. Fit the wall. Free to play, works offline.

Slot is a minimal one-touch reflex game. You are a shape, fixed near the bottom of the screen. Walls fall towards you and every wall has a hole cut in exactly your shape — turned to a different angle. One tap turns you 90°. Line up in time and you slide through. Get it wrong and you shatter.

How to play

  • Tap anywhere to turn 90° clockwise. That is the entire control scheme.
  • A square fits any way up. A bar has two answers. A T has only one.
  • Finish the turn before the wall reaches you and the fit counts CLEAN — double points.
  • Walls speed up, gaps shrink, and later walls add decoy holes and double layers.

Is this a gambling app?

No. Despite the name, Slot has no slot machine, no wheel, no cards, no coins, no virtual currency and no element of chance. "Slot" here means the shaped opening you have to fit through. The only thing that decides a run is your own timing.
