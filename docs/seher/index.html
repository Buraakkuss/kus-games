Seher

Prayer times, qibla compass, tasbih · v1.0.0

▶ Try it in your browser — no install, no account, works offline once loaded.

Most prayer-time apps ask for an internet connection to tell you something the sun already decided. Seher computes the times on your phone from the sun's position, so it works on a plane, in a basement, and in a country whose roaming you did not buy.

What it does

  • Six times a day, and a countdown to the next one. İmsak, Güneş, Öğle, İkindi, Akşam, Yatsı — with a progress bar showing how much of the current interval is gone.
  • Notifications per prayer. Turn each one on or off separately. Scheduled on the device, seven days ahead, so they fire with no connection.
  • Qibla compass. The great-circle bearing to the Kaaba, plus the distance. It vibrates once when you are facing it.
  • Tasbih counter. 33, 99 or open-ended, with a progress ring and a short vibration at the target. The count survives closing the app.
  • The ninety-nine names, with their reading and Turkish meaning.

Getting the times to match your local calendar

Seven calculation methods are built in (Diyanet, Muslim World League, ISNA, Egypt, Karachi, Umm al-Qura, Tehran), and Asr can follow either the Shafi'i or the Hanafi shadow ratio.

Official calendars usually add a few minutes of safety margin — temkin — to the astronomical result, and the amount differs by region. Seher has a per-prayer minute adjustment so you can line it up exactly with the calendar your mosque uses, and a one-tap preset that applies a typical temkin.

Ads

The app is free and shows an occasional full-screen ad. It will never show one within ten minutes of a prayer time, and never while the qibla compass or the counter is open. A one-off in-app purchase removes ads permanently.

Privacy

Your location is used on the device to compute the times and the qibla direction. It is not sent anywhere by us — there is no account, no server and no login. The advertising SDK is the only third party involved; what it collects and how to control it is in the Privacy Policy.
