Support

Seher · prayer times, qibla, tasbih

Write to kusgrupgames@gmail.com and you will get an answer. Include your device, your OS version, your city and — if the question is about a time — which calculation method you have selected. That is usually enough to answer in one reply.

The times differ from my mosque's calendar by a few minutes

That is expected, and the App has a control for it. Religious authorities add a safety margin called temkin to the astronomical result, and the amount differs by region and by prayer.

Open Settings → Minute adjustment and enter the difference for each prayer (positive to make it later, negative to make it earlier). Or tap "Temkin payı ekle" for a typical set and adjust from there. Compare against your calendar for one day and the App will then match it every day.

The times are wrong by an hour

That is a time-zone problem, not a calculation one.

  • If you picked a city from the list, the App uses that city's own time zone, including its daylight-saving changes. The times you see are that city's local times — which is what you want when you are checking another city, but note they are not converted to your phone's clock.
  • If you used "Find my location" or typed coordinates, the App uses your phone's current time zone. Check that your phone's date, time and time zone are set to automatic, then reopen the App.

If you have travelled and your phone has not yet picked up the new time zone, the times will follow the old one until it does.

The compass points the wrong way

  • Hold the phone flat, parallel to the ground.
  • Move away from metal furniture, speakers, magnets, laptops and cars. A magnetic phone case or holder is the most common cause.
  • Calibrate the magnetometer: move the phone in a figure-of-eight a few times.
  • If your phone has no magnetometer, the dial will not rotate. The number under it is still correct — use it with any compass.

Notifications do not arrive

  • Check that the prayer is switched on in Settings → Notifications in the App, and that notifications are allowed for Seher in your phone's system settings.
  • Android: exclude Seher from battery optimisation. Some manufacturers (Xiaomi, Huawei, Oppo, Samsung) stop background alarms aggressively; allow "auto start" and "no restrictions" for the App.
  • Do not force-stop the App from the task manager — that cancels its scheduled alarms until you open it again.
  • Open the App at least once a week. It schedules seven days ahead each time you open it.

Location

You never have to grant location permission. Pick your city from the list, or type your coordinates in Settings. Everything works the same way. Wherever the coordinates come from, they are used on the phone and are never sent anywhere — see the Privacy Policy.

I bought something and it is not active

Tap Settings → Restore purchase while signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with. If it still does not restore, write to us with the store receipt and we will sort it out.

What is the difference between "Remove ads" and Seher Pro?

  • Remove ads — a single payment, no renewal. It turns off advertising and nothing else.
  • Seher Pro — a monthly or yearly subscription. It unlocks every greeting card and every lesson in the alphabet section, and also removes advertising while it is active.

Cancel a subscription in your App Store or Google Play account, not in the app — that is where Apple and Google keep the controls. Cancelling keeps it active until the end of the period you already paid for.

Sharing a card does not work

  • The Share button opens your phone's own share sheet; the apps listed there are the ones installed on your phone. If WhatsApp or Instagram is missing from the list, it is not installed or it has been restricted in system settings.
  • Instagram cannot receive a direct "share to feed" from other apps on every version of the OS. If it does not appear, use Kaydet and then post the saved image from Instagram itself.
  • Kaydet writes the card into your phone's files (not the photo gallery). To put it in the gallery, use Share and pick "Save image" / "Fotoğraflara kaydet".

Is the Qur'an itself in the app?

Not in this version, and neither is a translation, a commentary or recitation audio. The learning section covers the alphabet, tecvid and a glossary of terms; the drills and examples are constructed to demonstrate a rule and are not Qur'anic text.

There are two reasons, and both are deliberate. First, scripture typeset from memory is unacceptable — a single wrong diacritic is a real error. Second, published translations and commentaries are copyrighted works belonging to their authors and publishers; we will not carry them without a licence. When they are added, it will be with a properly licensed text and the source named in the app.

The missed-prayer count went up on its own

That is how it works: anything you did not mark as prayed is added to your missed count when the day turns over in your selected location's timezone. Marking a prayer only affects today; your missed counts are separate and you can correct them by hand with the − and + buttons at any time.

I want a reminder before the prayer, not at it

Settings → "Vaktin kaç dakika öncesinde de haber verilsin?" and pick 10, 15 or 30 minutes. You then get two notifications for every prayer you have switched on: one ahead of time and one at the time itself.

Are the zakat figures binding?

No. It is a calculator, not a fatwa. Scholars differ on the nisab measure and on which debts may be deducted; follow the ruling of the authority you follow and use the calculator as arithmetic. Nothing you type is sent anywhere — the gram price is typed by you, not fetched.

I saw an inappropriate advert

Please write to us with a screenshot. Ad content in this app is limited to the "General audiences" rating and the gambling, betting, dating and sexual-content categories are blocked, but an advertiser occasionally slips through a classification. We block them individually as they are reported. If you would rather see none at all, Seher Pro and the one-off "Remove ads" purchase both turn advertising off completely.

Which calculation method should I pick?

  • Türkiye: Diyanet.
  • Europe, most of Asia, Africa: Muslim World League.
  • North America: ISNA.
  • Saudi Arabia: Umm al-Qura.
  • Pakistan, India, Bangladesh, Afghanistan: Karachi.
  • Egypt and much of the Levant: Egyptian General Authority.

For Asr, pick Hanafî if you follow the Hanafi school; otherwise leave it on Şâfiî, which is what Diyanet uses.

Does it work offline?

Yes, entirely. The times, the qibla direction, the counter and the notifications all work with no connection. Only the ads need the internet — with none, no ad is shown.
