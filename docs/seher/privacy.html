Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026 · Seher (iOS and Android)

This policy explains what happens to data when you use Seher. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

1. The short version

We do not ask for your name, e-mail or phone number, and there is no account. We run no servers, so your location and your settings never reach us — they are used on your phone and stay there. The one third party involved is Google's advertising SDK, which serves the ads that keep the app free. Buying "Remove ads" switches that off for good.

2. Who we are

The app is published by the developer named on the app's store listing ("we", "us"). Contact: kusgrupgames@gmail.com. For privacy questions, write to that address with "Privacy" in the subject.

3. Location

Prayer times and the qibla direction depend on where you are, so the app needs a latitude and a longitude. You can give it in three ways, and all three are handled the same way:

  • Device location. If you tap "Find my location", the operating system gives the app a coarse position. The app uses it to compute the times and the bearing, and saves it in local device storage so it does not have to ask again.
  • City list. Pick a city and the app uses that city's stored coordinates. No location permission is involved.
  • Typed coordinates. Enter latitude and longitude by hand.

Your location is never transmitted by us. The calculation is arithmetic performed on your phone; there is no lookup, no API call and no server to receive it. You can use the app with location permission permanently denied — the city list and manual entry cover everything.

4. Data stored on your device only

Seher saves the following in local device storage. It never leaves your phone, we cannot read it, and deleting the app deletes it:

  • The coordinates and city name you chose
  • Calculation method, Asr preference and your per-prayer minute adjustments
  • Which prayer notifications are on
  • Vibration and keep-screen-on preferences
  • Your tasbih count and target
  • Which alphabet lessons you have completed
  • Whether "Remove ads" or Seher Pro is active

5. Notifications

If you turn on notifications for a prayer, the app schedules local notifications on your device for the next seven days and refreshes them when you open it. They are produced by your phone, not pushed from a server. No notification token is created and nothing is sent to us. You can turn each prayer off in the app, or all of them in the system settings.

6. Advertising

The free version shows occasional full-screen ads through Google AdMob. To serve them, Google may process your device's advertising identifier, IP address (which implies a coarse, city-level location), device and OS details, and interaction with the ad. This is Google's processing, under Google's policy, not ours:

  • Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Your prayer times, your chosen coordinates, your settings and your tasbih count are not shared with the advertising SDK. It receives only what the platform gives every advertising SDK, listed above.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland you are shown a consent form (Google's UMP) before personalised ads are used, and you can reopen it any time from Settings → About → Ad privacy settings. On iOS, personalised ads also require App Tracking Transparency permission, which you can refuse; the app works identically if you do.

7. Purchases and subscriptions

"Remove ads" is a one-off purchase, and Seher Pro is an auto-renewing subscription (monthly or yearly). Both are handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see your card, your billing address or your store account — we run no payment system and hold no billing record. The store tells the app only whether the purchase or subscription is currently active. While it is, the advertising SDK is not initialised at all.

Cancelling or refunding is done in your App Store or Google Play account, not by us.

7a. Greeting cards and sharing

Cards are drawn on your device from text and shapes built into the app. Nothing is downloaded, and no card you view is reported anywhere.

When you tap Share, the app writes the card as an image file into your device's own temporary storage and hands it to your operating system's share sheet. You then choose the app to send it to. We do not see which card you chose, who you sent it to, or whether you sent it at all. Save writes the same image into your device's files and nothing more.

7b. Learning progress

Which lessons you have finished is stored on your device along with the rest of your settings. It is not sent anywhere and we cannot see it.

8. What we do not do

  • No account, no login, no sign-up
  • No analytics SDK of our own, no crash-reporting SDK, no social network SDK
  • No microphone, camera, contacts, photos, files or calendar access — the app requests none of these permissions
  • No profile of your religious practice: how often you open the app, which prayers you enable and what you count are never collected
  • No selling of personal data to anyone, ever

9. Children

Seher is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect anything from them. The app asks for no personal information from anyone.

10. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or erase. Everything the app keeps is on your device: clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it removes all of it. For data held by Google as an advertising provider, use the links in section 6 or your device's advertising settings.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it and the new version is published here before the change ships.
