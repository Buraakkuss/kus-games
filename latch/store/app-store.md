## Description (max 4000)
```
Latch is a one-touch reflex game about a single decision: when to let go.

Hold anywhere and a rope shoots to the nearest hook above you. You swing. The instant you release, you fly off along the arc you were travelling — and gravity takes it from there. Catch the next hook and keep going. Miss it and the drop below ends the run.

HOW IT WORKS
• Hold to throw the rope. Release to let go. That is the whole control scheme.
• A gold arc lights up on your swing circle — that is the release window at 45 degrees.
• Let go inside it for a PERFECT throw: double points.
• Release too early and you fall short. Too late and your speed points straight up: you stall and drop.
• Pick EASY, NORMAL or HARD from the menu. Each one keeps its own best score.

IT GETS MEANER
• Hooks drift further apart and your rope reaches less far.
• The PERFECT window narrows until it is a sliver.
• Spikes hang under the lazy line — a low, slow arc finds them.
• Dashed hooks snap after about a second and throw you whether you are ready or not.

EVERY RUN IS FAIR
Hooks are not scattered at random. Before a hook is placed, the game simulates its own physics to confirm a well-timed player can reach it, and every build is tested by a pilot that plays thousands of runs without dying once. If you fall, it was your timing — not the level.

BUILT TO RESPECT YOU
• Three difficulty modes — EASY widens the window and keeps the guide on, HARD gives you a sliver and nothing else.
• Works fully offline. No internet needed to play.
• No account. No sign-up. No e-mail. No social login.
• No forced videos between every run.
• One optional purchase removes ads forever. No subscriptions, no coins, no energy timers, no loot boxes.
• Tiny download, runs on old devices.

Free to play. Rewarded video is optional and only appears if you choose to continue a run.

How far can you get before your thumb lets go a fraction too late?
```

## App Review notları
```
No account or login is required. All features are immediately available.

Controls: press and hold anywhere to attach a rope to the nearest hook above the player and swing; release to let go. The gold arc drawn on the swing circle marks the optimal release window.

In-app purchase: "remove_ads" (non-consumable) removes interstitial advertising. It can be tested with a sandbox account; there is also a "Restore purchase" option under Menu -> i.

Ads: Google AdMob. Rewarded video is optional and user-initiated (the "Watch ad, continue" button after a run ends).

The app uses App Tracking Transparency; declining the prompt is fully supported and results in non-personalised ads.
```
