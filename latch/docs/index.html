Latch

Hold to swing. Let go at the right moment. Free to play, works offline.

Latch is a one-touch reflex game about a single decision: when to let go. Hold anywhere and a rope shoots to the nearest hook above you. You swing. The instant you release, you fly off along the arc you were travelling — and gravity takes it from there.

How to play

  • Hold anywhere to throw the rope. Release to let go. That is the whole control scheme.
  • A gold arc appears on your swing circle: that is the release window at 45°.
  • Let go inside it and the throw counts PERFECT — double points.
  • Too early and you fall short. Too late and you go straight up, then straight down.
  • Miss the next hook and the drop below ends the run.

Every run is fair

Hooks are not scattered at random. Before a hook is placed, the game simulates its own physics to confirm a well-timed player can actually reach it — and a test pilot plays thousands of runs on every build to prove no impossible section exists. If you fall, it was your timing, not the level.
